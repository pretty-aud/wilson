## Facts that will bite

- Move the untracked `.claude/launch.json` at the worktree root to your scratchpad before the checkout; never delete it. `node_modules` is absent in a fresh worktree.
- **`otterFetch` resolves for every status**: read the body before `ok`, never after; a hidden subject is a 404 on the wire (B1 made it so), never an empty 200, and §5.4's words come from the index's `state`, not from the 404.
- **A linked body lives in no session cache**, and the Validator is a session-long store rendered hidden and never unmounted; the quiz picker's linked rows come from `subjectShares`, not `quizSelectionData`.
- **The key, not the slug**: a linked `modifiers` must never light up or write under the host's own `modifiers`; `otter_progress.completed_lessons` is keyed by slug for own subjects and by `link:<subject_id>` for linked ones, on the **host** course's row.
- **The six save paths end differently** (two early returns after `await loadSoftwareList()`, two `selectSoftware`/`selectSubject` calls, two unawaited loads): the helper runs after the last save and waits on none of that; `subject.save`'s response carries no id, so ids come from `subject.list`; a previously declined or withdrawn subject is not new.
- **Refusals arrive verbatim as 409**; `not_a_workspace_member` is an identifier to word; a 500 is not a refusal (an expired session) and must not be shown as one.
- **A `gone` row inside a batch card** has exactly one control, Dismiss; Accept all skips it, Decline all takes it.
- **The Local Server shows nothing**: every control renders only in `cloudMode`; the routes answer 501 beneath; the fixtures tester at 5301 shows the chrome with an empty list, by design.
- **`shareDiscoverability.test.js` slices `otterRoutes.js` on an LF-only anchor**: on a CRLF checkout the slice runs to the end of the file; the share routes sit above the nominations block for that reason (B1's hand-off §5). Do not move them.
