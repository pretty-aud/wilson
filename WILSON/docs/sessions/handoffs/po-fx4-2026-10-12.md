   them with node, keeping the target's CRLF.

   all commit fine; a find/replace must match `\r\n`.
7. **The provider's undo history is not per person**: a test about "the
   next person gets no undo step of THIS save" must start from an empty

8. **With no dialog in front of it, Save edit is one press and two round
   trips while the cut, its keys and every exit stay live** (round 1, R1-01).
   Anything "after a save" must settle the draft it wrote, not the draft the
   list holds by then.

   never answers then holds `leaveGuard`'s one-question lock and every exit

   it settle itself.

    the bar**: select the bar's own line by `.sr-only[role="status"]`.
11. **A disabled button cannot take focus, and Chromium blurs a focused

    `blur()` on a disabled button). Move focus once the save settles; in a
    test, lose focus by removing a focused element.
12. **A hidden announcement starting "Saved …" collided with every script

13. **`src/dev/devFixtures.test.js` allows only listed pure modules in the
