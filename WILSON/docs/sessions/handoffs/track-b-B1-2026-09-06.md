## 5. Traps hit

1. 🚨 **"Take the LAST X-Forwarded-For hop" is WRONG on Supabase.** TPN-NET-004
   prescribed it and the old `provision-workspace` carried it. Measured on
   wilson-dev with a throwaway header-echo function: the chain is
   `<client via Cloudflare>, <client via the AWS balancer>, <13.248.0.0/14
   relay>`; a caller-supplied `x-forwarded-for` or `x-real-ip` is STRIPPED;
   a spoofed `cf-connecting-ip` is answered by Cloudflare with a 403. Keyed on
   the last hop (dev v7, eleven minutes), 22 requests spread over ten limiter
   rows and nothing was refused — and a relay's pooled counter would refuse
   every customer behind it. Use `cf-connecting-ip`. The burst probe with a
   failing control is what caught it; a green deploy would not have.
