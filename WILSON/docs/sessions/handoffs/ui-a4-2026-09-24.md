## 5. Traps hit

1. 🚨 **THE KIT DRAWER IS Z-60, SO A HAND-ROLLED OVERLAY IT OPENS APPEARS
   BEHIND IT.** The settings slide-out opens Import and Clear all data, and
   both were `fixed inset-0 z-50` boxes. On the first attempt (the Drawer
   first) the walk reported that the clear confirm "did not open". It had
   opened, under the Drawer and its backdrop. Convert every overlay a Drawer
   opens onto the Dialog (z-70) BEFORE the Drawer, or no intermediate commit
   is correct. A4's nine commits are ordered that way.
2. 🚨 **THE APP'S BROWSER PANE, WHEN HIDDEN, NEVER FINISHES A TRANSITION AND
   THROTTLES ITS TIMERS.** `getComputedStyle` read a disabled ghost Button's
   old ink, and a chevron at 0deg, a full second after the state changed. A
   probe with a 5s `setTimeout` timed out at 45s, and
   `document.visibilityState` was `hidden`. Measure in headless Playwright, or
   switch transitions off in the probe, and say which.
3. 🚨 **`authSelectors.test.js` WATCHES EVERY ARIA-LABEL IN THE APP.** A new
   name for the share dialog's note, "Why this should be the company
   standard", also answered `getByLabel('Company')`, and the full suite failed
   after a merge while the O.T.T.E.R. tests alone were green. Run the whole
   suite after naming anything.
4. ⚠️ **`shareDiscoverability.test.js` READS ANY otter.css RULE THAT NAMES
   `.ui-iconbtn` AS ONE THAT CAN REACH THE ROW MENU'S TRIGGER.** A rotate on
   the quiz's expand chevron failed it as "transformed". Style an IconButton's
   glyph through your own class alone (`.otter-quiz-expand > svg`).
5. ⚠️ **THE COLOUR GUARD COUNTS `text-decoration` AS A COLOUR PROPERTY.**
   Write an underline as `text-decoration-line: underline`.
6. ⚠️ **`Dialog` DROPPED A CALLER'S `aria-label` WHEN ITS TITLE WAS A NODE**
   (fixed, A4-KR-1). A `sr-only` span named by `aria-labelledby` works too, but
   the walk counts its 1px box as a clipped element.
7. ⚠️ **`position: fixed` HAS NO `offsetParent`.** A probe that tests
   visibility with `offsetParent !== null` never finds a Drawer, a Dialog's
   backdrop or any fixed layer. Use `getClientRects().length > 0`.
8. ⚠️ **THE SHELL'S NAV STRIP HOLDS "Tool settings"**, and an exact-text
   search finds it before the Drawer's own "Tool settings" tab. Click the tab
   through the Drawer (`[role=tab]` inside `.otter-settings-drawer`).
9. ⚠️ **MODULES CAN BE REWRITTEN IN FLIGHT FOR STATES THE FIXTURES CANNOT
   REACH**, without touching the source: `page.route` on the module's
   pathname, `route.fetch()`, a string replace, then `route.fulfill`. A4 used
   it for:
   - `aiProxy.js`'s `callAI`, answered from `globalThis` (the quiz, the
     Validator);
   - the fixture course, given a source course (Suggest a change) or made a
     coding language (code writing);
   - the fixture routes' `trash.list`, `cr.list` and `nomination.list`,
     filled;
   - a forced `useState` initialiser (the unreachable duplicate dialog, the
     settings error).
10. 📌 **THE HARNESS ECHOES A WHOLE SCRATCH FILE BACK WHENEVER IT CHANGES ON
    DISK.** Write scratch files once, and make follow-up edits in the target.
11. 📌 **`git apply --3way` ON A SET-ASIDE PATCH** is how A4 reordered the
    settings commit behind the dialogs it opens. It conflicts wherever the
    intervening commits touched the same lines; resolve by union.
12. 📌 The fixtures' change requests, nominations, editor grants and trash
    restores REFUSE writes by design (`devWriteRefused`), which exercises
    every error path of Requests, Share and Trash for free.
13. 🚨 **A SCROLLER WITH NOTHING FOCUSABLE IN IT IS A TAB STOP** in Chromium
    130+, Electron 33 included, and its ring is the global one, drawn outside.
    The Validator's queue took focus with only 2px of its ring showing:
    - the panel body clipped the sides;
    - the next list's sticky head covered the bottom;
    - drawn inside, its own sticky head covered the top.

    A sticky head belongs ABOVE a list that can take focus, not inside it.
14. 🚨 **TAB SCROLLS THE NEXT CONTROL UNTIL IT TOUCHES THE SCROLLER'S EDGE.**
    A ring drawn outside (2px plus a 1px offset) falls past the edge whenever
    a view scrolls: 0px of its bottom showed on three controls at 1280x700,
    and none at 1440x900. Use `scroll-padding-block: 4px` on the scroller
    (A4-KR-9 puts it on the kit Dialog's body). Measure focus with a REAL Tab
    at the smaller size.
15. ⚠️ **`:is()` WEIGHS AS ITS HEAVIEST ARGUMENT.** Putting
    `.otter-settings-card .ui-card-title` into the locked rule's `:is()` would
    have raised the label's selector from (0,3,0) to (0,4,0) and tied its
    hover lift. Write a selector LIST when the alternatives weigh differently.
16. ⚠️ **A GUARD THAT READS "ANY VALUE" ACCEPTS EVERY STATE MUTANT.** Round
    1's state guard took any attribute written from a variable, a field or a
    call as able to hold every value. 24 generated states lived, and F1, F3,
    F4 and F7 with them. `otterCss.test.js`'s `valuesOf` works the values out
    instead:
    - a const through its initialiser;
    - `useState` through its setter's calls;
    - a local function through its returns;
    - a test as true / false, a prop default as false-y.

    A value read from data may only be a word the file names. The kit side
    (`KIT`, `elementAttr`) reads every `src/ui/*.jsx` with the props each use
