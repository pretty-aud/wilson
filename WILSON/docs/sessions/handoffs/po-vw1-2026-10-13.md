## 5. Traps hit

1. **`npm install` can fail on a TLS fault** (`ERR_SSL_CIPHER_OPERATION_FAILED`);
   a plain retry passed. Check `node_modules` before reading a test failure.
2. **A toolbar's room is not "Search's end to Export's start".** The right
   slot begins with the count, and the slots keep an 8px gap: measure the
   slots' boxes (`.ui-toolbar-slot`), not two controls (review R2-1 caught
   102px that was 42px). And a toolbar whose last button sits at the content
   edge may still be on two lines: measure its height.
3. **A selector "class contains menu" stops at `rb-task-menu-label`.** Match
   a class TOKEN that ends in `menu` (`/(^|-)menu$/`); each lane has its own
   (`rb-task-menu`, `rb-asset-menu`, `rb-scene-menu`, `rb-ent-menu`).
4. **A native select's open list never reaches a screenshot** (the OS draws
   it): record its options in the measurement JSON instead.
5. **The desktop's page address changes at every launch** (`listen(0)`), so
   anything kept in the browser's storage most likely starts empty each
   launch (OUTSTANDING). The design keeps "last used" in `user_settings`
   (cloud) and the machine's settings file (desktop), never `localStorage`.
6. **Suite 100 is the first three-digit suite:** the coverage guard is a
   shell glob (`ls …/[0-9][0-9]_"${t}".sql`), `tap-all.mjs` sorts suites as
   text, and CI's replay list is written out by hand: teach all three in the
   suite's commit (the design's §5.2 has the `compgen` form).
7. **`fn_audit_touch` stamps only what the client leaves empty**, on insert
   too: a client can post its own `created_by` and a 2099 `updated_at`. Pin
   them in a trigger (the design's §5.2).
8. **A red Vitest job's log needs sign-in, its annotations do not**:
   `GET /repos/pretty-aud/wilson/check-runs/<job id>/annotations` names the
   failing test.
9. **A free-text answer can answer a different aspect than the one asked**
   (her Q5a described the card's contents, not its place): ask the open
   aspect again, with a picture, rather than assume (Q5b).
10. **Her "i can see the levels or scenes or shots related"** read as *can't*
    from the code (no such column existed); asked (Q11), not assumed.
11. **A reviewer's interim choice can contradict her own answers** (R2-2:
    greying controls she had answered should be for editors only). Check an
    interim choice against the verbatim answers before building it.
12. **FX10 merged while VW1 ran** (`fde81844`, merged here in `86cfa233`):
    the research's column widths predate it (the Tasks and Assets date
    columns are now 144px, the Shots dates 150px; the research's §2.3 says
    so). Re-measure before relying on a width; the toolbar numbers are
    unchanged.
