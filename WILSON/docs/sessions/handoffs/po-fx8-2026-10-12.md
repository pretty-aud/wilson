## 5. Traps hit

1. **The worktree's untracked `.claude/launch.json` blocks the checkout**:
   moved to `scratchpad/launch-json-moved/` (never deleted); the tracked one
   gains `fx8-worktree` (5296).
2. **Chromium's container query units ignore the scrollbar**: with a
   vertical scrollbar `100cqw` is 10px wider than the table's 100%. The
   auto column absorbs it (the title is its maximum less the scrollbar while
   a list scrolls); a minimum must leave the scrollbar's room. Playwright's
   Chromium HIDES scrollbars by default (`--hide-scrollbars`): measure with
   `ignoreDefaultArgs: ['--hide-scrollbars']` (`fx8-shots.mjs --scrollbars`).
   The 10px is Chromium's `scrollbar-width: thin`; the Electron build's
   own width was not measured (Tasks allows 18).
3. **A `<button>` sizes to its words, not to its box**: moved out of a flex
   parent into a block box, the Assets name button ran under its row's
   buttons with no ellipsis (R1-01). Every lane's words must fill the
   words box (`width: 100%` or `calc(100% + 6px)` beside a −6px margin).
4. **A lane rule that hides a HoverActions slot outranks the kit's
   `(hover: none)` reveal** (the nest's isolation, R2-01): put it inside
   `@media (hover: hover)`; `rabbitRowTitles.test.jsx` refuses one outside.
5. **The kit Menu takes focus and the pointer into its portal**, so a
   HoverActions slot holding a ⋯ faded while its menu was open (R2-03).
6. **`kitFights` reads `.ui-row-title > .ui-hover-actions` as reaching every
   HoverActions** (the Relations panel's): key a kit rule on the slot's own
   class (`.ui-row-title-acts`). `KIT_ROOT` gains `RowTitle`.
7. **`onlyWidths` in `rabbitEntityViewsRender` accepts only one-word
   `--rb-ent-<word>` widths** (`--rb-ent-desc`, not `--rb-ent-col-desc`).
8. **Bash heredocs halve `\\`** (as the brief said): a `\'` in a test name
   became `'` and broke the file; scripts with escapes go through Write.
9. **An `npx vitest run` without `--maxWorkers=4`** failed about thirty
   Timeline tests under load; capped, every run passed.
10. **A reviewer can leave things in the scratchpad** (round 2 made a copy
    with a `node_modules` junction and removed it): check `git status`,
    HEAD and `node_modules` after each round, and read a reviewer's script
    before running it.
