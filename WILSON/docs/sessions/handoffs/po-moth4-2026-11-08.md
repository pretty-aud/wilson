## 5. Traps hit

- **SQL does not short-circuit a cast**: compare `conrelid = to_regclass('x')`, not `'x'::regclass` (MO3's, again).
- **A trigger's order is its name's**: on files `trg_files_body_guard` runs before `trg_files_folder_trash_guard`.
- **A column trigger fires on the UPDATE's own target list**, not on a BEFORE trigger's change: the epoch moves
  inside `fn_projects_owner_guard`, so 0132's epoch trigger fires on any UPDATE with a WHEN.
- **A transition table's row is a record, not the table's type**: pass ids and read the rows again.
- **One transaction's realtime messages share their moment**: compare them sorted, never by `inserted_at`.
- **A row trigger that checks a parent during a batch UPDATE sees the batch half done**: the restore's own rows must
  not be asked (the round's blocker).
- **A project's maker is seated on it** (`fn_projects_auto_staff`): another member needs a `project_members` row.
- **Any active member reads a shared project's board** through Moth's gate (privacy and the personal arm, never a seat).
- **Restating `folders_select` hides the trash from UPDATE and DELETE too** (they see only what they read), but a
  cascade from a live ancestor still reaches it: the trash guard's DELETE arm.
- **A reviewer's reading cost about 1.5 points of the weekly meter for 2.76M tokens**; this session's own work about 1
  point per 100k of new context. Plan rounds by the meter.
