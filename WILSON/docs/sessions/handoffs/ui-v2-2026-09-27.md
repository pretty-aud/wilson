   1440 walk made five of its screens miss their timing (otter-share,
   rabbit-task-history, three Experiences screens); all five open alone.
   One browser job at a time against the dev server.

   `PROBE_PATH=/settings` reached the probe as
   `C:/Program Files/Git/settings`. `export MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1`, then pass
   file arguments in Windows form (`C:/Users/…`), because `/c/Users/…`
   is no longer converted either.

   guard failed on the word "brightness" in the comment explaining the
