## F10 — R.A.B.B.I.T.'s twins

**What changed underneath.** R.A.B.B.I.T.'s Scenes, Shots, Levels, Experiences, Assets and Tasks pages each carried
their own copy of a few small pieces — the text you click to edit in a table or a window, the bulk bar's pickers,
the "Delete?" question — and their own copy of the code around the saved views, and the program kept four
near-identical sets of "save a scene / shot / level / experience" routines. Those copies had drifted apart, so a
fix made in one place was missing in the others. Now there is one of each, shared: the kit's editor, bulk picker and
question; one piece behind every table's saved views, filters and sorts; one set of save routines for the four
kinds; one piece behind the windows of scenes, shots, levels and experiences, and one for setting or removing their
pictures. Nothing new is on the screen; the pages look as they did.

**What you may notice (all small):**
1. After you edit a name or a title in a table or a window and press Enter or Escape, the keyboard stays on that
   name, so the next Tab goes on from there. Before, it jumped back to the top of the page.
2. In the Tasks and Assets bulk bars, picking **None** for Phase (and, on Tasks, for Assignee) now clears it on the
   ticked rows. Before, picking None did nothing.
3. In a Level's or an Experience's window, after Save, Cancel or Escape in Description or Notes, the keyboard stays on
   the words; and if a teammate changes the description while you have it open but haven't typed, you now see their
   words (once you have typed, yours stay).

**How it was measured.** Before and after, by counting the copies and their lines and by the test suite (14,600
tests): each change above has a test that failed on the old code and passes now, and the pages' own tests, the
permission checks and the speed checks (how often the screen redraws) pass without being changed.

**To try, if you like (nothing needs checking):** rename a scene in the Scenes table and press Enter, then Tab; in
Tasks, tick two tasks and pick Phase → None.

**Questions for you** (asked in F10's chat; the recommended answer first; until you answer, nothing changes):
1. Should the Levels and Experiences windows ask "Discard your changes?" before throwing away typed text, as Scenes
   and Shots do? a) Yes (recommended) b) No, keep as is.
2. Should the Levels and Experiences windows take the Scene window's layout? a) Keep their own (recommended) b) Yes.
3. Should Levels and Experiences get the # tags and [[ links that Scenes and Shots have? a) Not now (recommended) b) Yes.
